Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Sign up as a worker or an employer, complete your profile, then post a job or apply to one.

How does payment work?

Jobs have a budget in KES. When an employer marks the work complete, the amount is credited to the worker's wallet and recorded as a transaction and invoice.

How are workers and employers vetted?

Every completed job ends with a two-way review, so ratings reflect real work. Profiles show skills, rates and history.

What kind of work is on Kibarua?

Everyday casual jobs (cleaning, house help, plumbing, carpentry, painting, gardening, cooking, moving) and skilled work (web & mobile development, design, writing, data analysis, accounting), remote or on-site.
