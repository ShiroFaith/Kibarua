About Kibarua

Empowering Kenya's Informal Workforce

Our Mission

Kibarua is revolutionizing the casual labor market in Kenya by creating a centralized, transparent platform that connects job seekers with employers. We believe every worker deserves fair pay, safe working conditions, and dignity in their work.

Our Values

Trust

We build trust between employers and workers through transparent communication and fair practices.

Safety

Worker safety and well-being are our top priorities in every job we facilitate.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in every service we provide to our community.
