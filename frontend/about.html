About Kibarua

Connecting Kenya's workforce with the people who need it.

What is Kibarua?

Kibarua ("casual work" in Swahili) is an online marketplace that connects Kenyan employers with casual and skilled workers. Employers post gigs of every kind, from everyday casual jobs like cleaning, house help, plumbing, gardening and cooking to skilled work like web development, design and writing, and workers apply with a proposal and their price. Once hired, both sides coordinate through in-app messaging, the worker is paid in KES on completion, and each engagement ends with a review that builds a trusted reputation over time.

Our goal is simple: make finding work and hiring help fast, transparent and fair - with clear budgets, direct communication, and no guesswork.

How Kibarua Works

1. Post or Apply

Employers post a job with a budget and skills. Workers search, filter and apply with a bid and cover letter.

2. Hire & Collaborate

Employers compare applicants, hire the best fit, and coordinate the work through in-app chat.

3. Complete & Review

On completion the worker's KES wallet is credited, an invoice is recorded, and both sides leave a rating.
