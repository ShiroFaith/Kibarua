Work. Hire. Get Paid.

Kibarua is Kenya's marketplace for gig work - where employers post jobs and casual & skilled workers apply, get hired, chat, and get paid in KES.

Find Work Hire Talent

Everything you need to work or hire

Post & Find Jobs

Employers post gigs with a budget and required skills. Workers search and filter by category, pay, location and experience.

Apply with a Bid

Send a proposal with a cover letter and your price. Employers compare applicants and hire the right person.

Chat In-App

Message employers and workers directly to agree on scope, timelines and details before work begins.

Wallet & Payments

When a job is completed, earnings are settled to the worker's KES wallet, with a transaction record and invoice.

Smart Recommendations

Workers get job matches scored against their skills, so the best opportunities come to them.

Ratings & Reviews

Both sides review each engagement. Reputation is built on real, completed work.

How It Works

1

Create an Account

Sign up as a worker (job seeker) or an employer and set up your profile.

2

Post or Apply

Employers post a job; workers browse, search and apply with a bid and cover letter.

3

Hire & Do the Work

Employers review applicants, hire, and coordinate through in-app messaging.

4

Complete & Get Paid

On completion, the worker's wallet is credited in KES and both sides leave a review.
